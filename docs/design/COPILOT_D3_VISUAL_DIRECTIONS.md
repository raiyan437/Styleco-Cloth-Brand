## Current selection: Direction A — Editorial Pop

Direction A is the recommended and selected direction for D4. It best matches the
approved reference language while supporting clear product discovery. Usability
guardrails: reserve bright accents for campaign emphasis and focus states, anchor
body copy on neutral surfaces, keep product cards quiet, and make asymmetry serve
hierarchy rather than novelty.

This selection is a working visual direction, not Design Freeze. D5–D7 should test
the system with the Header and Categories before committing later pages. The hero is
retained as a future campaign option rather than a current homepage module.
